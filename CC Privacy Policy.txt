PRIVACY POLICY FOR MOBILE CYCLE COUNT
Effective Date: October 19, 2026

1. INTRODUCTION
"Mobile Cycle Count" is a mobile application developed by Tridex Systems and integrated with an organization's Infor CloudSuite environment.
The App is intended solely for authorized employees of organizations that deploy Mobile Cycle Count. It enables employees to access, manage, and execute real-time inventory counting operations across warehouse, retail, and counter sales environments.
This Privacy Policy explains what information Mobile Cycle Count collects, how it is used, where it is stored, and how it is protected.
____________________________________
2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
Mobile Cycle Count collects and processes only the information necessary to perform warehouse and retail inventory counting operations.
Employee Information:
Name
Company email address
Organization or company information
Business Information:
Depending on how the App is configured and used by the customer organization, Mobile Cycle Count processes business information stored within the organization's Infor CloudSuite / SX.e environment, including:
Inventory counts, item quantities, and physical bin/counter locations
Count configurations and user display settings (e.g., toggles for hiding or showing "Quantity Expected")
Saved work-in-progress count data
Device and Diagnostic Information:
Mobile Cycle Count may collect limited device and application diagnostic information, including:
Device hardware details required for display optimization across different screen sizes (such as tablets and mobile phones) and dynamic column views
Application error logs
Diagnostic information generated by the Infor Mongoose platform
In certain error scenarios, diagnostic logs may contain business-related information necessary to investigate and resolve application issues.
____________________________________
3. AUTHENTICATION
Users authenticate using their organization's Microsoft Single Sign-On (SSO) through Infor CloudSuite.
Mobile Cycle Count does not create independent user accounts and does not store user passwords. User permissions and visibility controls—such as group security rules dictating which columns and values (e.g., Quantity Expected) are displayed to specific employees—are governed through the customer's enterprise ERP setup.
____________________________________
4. PERMISSIONS USED
Mobile Cycle Count requests only the permissions required to provide its functionality.
Camera:
The camera may be used for functionality such as scanning barcode/QR code labels on inventory items, bin locations, or physical count tickets.
Other Permissions:
Mobile Cycle Count may request additional device permissions when necessary to provide specific functionality.
____________________________________
5. HOW INFORMATION IS STORED
Mobile Cycle Count does not store business or user information on servers owned or operated by Tridex Systems.
Instead:
Business and inventory count information is communicated in real-time and stored directly within the customer's Infor CloudSuite environment.
Diagnostic logs are stored within the customer's Infor Mongoose environment.
Mobile Cycle Count operates primarily online using active real-time connectivity. A temporary application cache (such as saved work-in-progress) on the device is maintained securely using protected storage mechanisms until synchronized, updated, or cleared.
Tridex Systems does not independently copy, export, sell, or maintain customer business data outside the customer's environment.
____________________________________
6. HOW INFORMATION IS USED
Information processed by Mobile Cycle Count is used solely to:
Authenticate authorized users.
Eliminate paper count sheets and manual double-entry by communicating inventory counts in real time with CloudSuite .
Render dynamic screen displays and column layouts customized for specific device forms (phones or tablets) and group security rules.
Allow employees to save work-in-progress counts and perform accurate cycle counting in warehouse, sales floor, and counter sales settings.
Troubleshoot application issues and maintain reliability across supported Android devices.
____________________________________
7. DATA SHARING
Mobile Cycle Count does not sell or share personal information with advertisers or unrelated third parties.
Information processed through the App is available only to:
The customer organization that owns and manages the relevant Infor CloudSuite environment.
Authorized support personnel when granted access by the customer for troubleshooting purposes.
Developer access to customer information is available only through customer-provided accounts and solely for authorized support and maintenance activities.
____________________________________
8. ANALYTICS AND DIAGNOSTICS
Mobile Cycle Count uses diagnostic capabilities provided by the Infor Mongoose platform to record application errors and assist with troubleshooting.
Mobile Cycle Count does not use third-party advertising or marketing services.
Diagnostic information is used strictly to support, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the application's reliability and performance.
____________________________________
9. SECURITY
Mobile Cycle Count communicates with Infor CloudSuite using secure encrypted connections.
Information cached locally on the device (such as offline work-in-progress) is protected and synchronized with the customer's Infor CloudSuite environment when connectivity is restored.
Access to customer information and diagnostic logs is controlled through the customer's Infor CloudSuite and Infor Mongoose security permissions.
While reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are implemented, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
____________________________________
10. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
Mobile Cycle Count integrates with services required to provide its functionality, including:
Microsoft authentication services for user sign-in
Infor CloudSuite APIs
Infor Mongoose platform services
No advertising networks or unrelated third-party services receive business information collected through the App.
____________________________________
11. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
Mobile Cycle Count is intended solely for authorized employees of customer organizations.
The App is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and Tridex Systems does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
